How to play

Touch and hold the left half of the screen for the left flipper and the right half for the right flipper. Use two fingers to flip both. When the ball is waiting in the plunger lane, hold the right half to pull the plunger back and let go to launch. Hold longer for more power.

The pop bumpers are Julius, Green and Haze. Roll through the top lanes to light B-R-E-W for a bigger score multiplier.

After a high score, tap the left or right side to change a letter and tap the middle to move on. With a keyboard, the arrow keys flip, Space launches and Enter starts a game.

Each note records your score, batch and ball, and stays on this iPad.
